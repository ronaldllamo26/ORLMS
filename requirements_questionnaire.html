Ordinance and Resolution Lifecycle Management System
Requirements Gathering Questionnaire
This document is intended to guide interviews with legislative office personnel to understand the current manual process and gather system requirements.
1. Ordinance Drafting and Encoding Module
  1. What metadata fields (e.g., Title, Author, Date, Sponsors) must be captured during the document encoding phase?
  2. What file formats (e.g., PDF, Word) do you currently attach or upload to each ordinance record?
  3. What standard numbering format is used for ordinances in your LGU (e.g., ORD-YYYY-001)?
2. Resolution Drafting and Submission Module
  1. How do you draft and submit resolutions, and how does this process differ from ordinances?
  2. What numbering format is used for resolutions in your LGU (e.g., RES-No-YYYY-001)?
  3. Should the system maintain a separate list and numbering sequence for resolutions compared to ordinances?
3. Review and Committee Endorsement Module
  1. How are submitted ordinances and resolutions referred to specific committees (e.g., Committee on Rules, Committee on Finance)?
  2. What is the process for committee chairpersons to document their review notes and recommendations?
  3. Should the system scan drafts against the database to check for duplicate or overlapping regulations?
4. Approval and Enactment Module
  1. How are council votes (Yes, No, Abstain) officially recorded for each ordinance or resolution?
  2. How is the Mayor's executive action (approval, veto, or pocket veto) logged in the system?
  3. What delays do you face when routing approved documents for signature and enactment?
5. Publication and Dissemination Module
  1. How are officially enacted ordinances and resolutions published for the public?
  2. What search filters (e.g., date range, keywords, categories) do citizens need to find laws?
  3. Would automatically generated plain-language summaries (English/Tagalog) help citizens understand complex laws?
6. Implementation Monitoring Module
  1. How do you currently track if enacted ordinances are being implemented or enforced?
  2. What statuses do you use to monitor implementation progress (e.g., Pending, Ongoing, Completed)?
  3. Who is responsible for logging operational updates and progress reports for each active law?
7. Amendment and Revision Tracking Module
  1. How do you document amendments, revisions, updates, or repeals of existing laws?
  2. How do you ensure that staff refer to the most updated version of an ordinance?
  3. Should the system display a version history showing side-by-side text comparisons of modifications?
